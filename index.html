About Sai Teja — Backend & AI Engineer

I'm Sai Teja — a backend and AI engineer with 3+ years building distributed systems, enterprise platforms, and AI-powered products. I care about writing clean, efficient code and understanding the system end-to-end, not just the layer I own.

Currently at Dhan AI, I've shipped across customer portals, internal operations tooling, and a large-scale multi-tenant CXP platform. I'm drawn to hard problems — event-driven architectures, microservice coordination, and systems that need to be resilient at scale.
